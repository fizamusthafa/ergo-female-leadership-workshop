# My priorities: knowledge file for my Chief-of-Staff agent

Illustrative figures for training purposes, not ERGO results

## Who I am
Miriam Kessler, Head of Customer Service, ERGO Customer Service Region West (fictional unit). About 120 people in six teams, phone, mail, chat and complaints. I report to the Director of Operations.

## My three priorities this quarter (Q4 2026)
1. **Phone waiting time.** Bring the average waiting time below 90 seconds by the end of December.
2. **Old complaints.** No complaint older than ten days by the end of the quarter.
3. **Callback service.** Decide on the rollout of the callback pilot to all teams and, if the answer is yes, plan it.

Anything that moves one of these three forward comes first. Anything else gets a polite "later" or goes to my team.

## Working rules
- Everything from my team is priority 1. Reply the same day, even if it's only "seen, I'll come back tomorrow".
- Urgent operational problems (systems down, queues out of control) beat everything else, including meetings.
- Board office requests: same day. If I can't give the full answer, send what I have and say when the rest comes.
- Meetings without an agenda: ask for one before accepting. Don't accept or decline for me.
- Vendors: no meetings this quarter unless it helps one of my three priorities. Draft a friendly "not now".
- Newsletters and all-staff mails: no reply needed. Only tell me if something affects my team.
- Favours from peers: I like to help, but check first if it clashes with my team's plans (for example our Saturday shifts).
- Drafts only. Never send anything on my behalf.

## Working hours
Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 16:30. No meetings before 09:00 and none after 16:00. Friday afternoons are for planning, so keep them free if possible.
