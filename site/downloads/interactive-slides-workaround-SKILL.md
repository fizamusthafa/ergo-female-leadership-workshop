# Make one slide interactive

Turn the slide I point to into a small clickable overview that viewers can explore on their own in Slide Show. Keep every fact and figure exactly as it is.

## Steps

1. Read the slide and pick its three to six main items (for example one per KPI, region or risk).
2. Turn the slide into an **overview**: one tile per item, laid out in a grid, each tile with a short label and its key number. Keep the slide title.
3. For each tile, add a **detail slide** right after the overview: a headline that states the message, the number, and two or three bullet points from the original slide or its speaker notes. Never add figures that are not in the deck.
4. Link each tile on the overview to its detail slide (an action or hyperlink to "this slide").
5. On every detail slide, add a small **"Back to overview"** link in the bottom right corner that goes back to the overview slide.
6. Hide the detail slides from the normal slide order only if I ask for it.

## Style

- Match the deck's existing fonts and colours. If the deck uses ERGO style, use one red accent (#BF1629) per slide.
- Tiles are flat rectangles with the same size and spacing. No shadows, no 3D.

## When you finish

In chat, list the overview slide number, the detail slides you created, and remind me to test it in Slide Show: clicking a tile opens the detail, "Back to overview" returns.
