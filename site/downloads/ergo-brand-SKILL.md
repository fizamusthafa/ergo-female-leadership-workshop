# Make slides look like an ERGO board deck

Restyle the open presentation. Keep every fact, figure and claim exactly as it is. You may reword titles into messages and split or shorten bullets, but never add, remove or change a number.

## Headlines

- Every content slide gets a full-sentence headline that states the "so what", for example "Growth ahead of plan, result held back by Property & Casualty". Maximum two lines, about 12 words.
- Topic labels such as "Q3 Results" or "P&C update" are only allowed on section dividers.

## Text

- Maximum 3 bullets per slide, each a complete thought of no more than 15 words. Move the rest into the speaker notes, not off the slide entirely.
- No exclamation marks, no underlining, no all-caps, no shadows or WordArt.
- Write "EUR 4,125m" or "€4.1bn", "93.4%", "+2.1 pts". Use the same format on every slide.

## Look

- White background. Text in charcoal `#242424`, secondary text in grey `#6B6B6B`.
- ERGO red `#BF1629` is the only accent colour. Use it once per slide, for the one thing that matters most (a key number, the focus bar in a chart, a thin rule).
- Font: Segoe UI (Segoe UI Semibold for titles). Title 28 pt, body 16-18 pt, footnotes 9 pt.
- Title top-left, ERGO logo top-right if the template provides it, generous margins, left-aligned text.
- If the deck uses the ERGO board template, apply its layouts instead of formatting by hand.

## Charts

- Flat 2D only. Replace 3D, pie-in-3D and exploding charts with a simple bar or column chart.
- No gridlines, no chart border. Label values directly; drop the legend when labels make it unnecessary.
- The current or focus series is ERGO red; comparison series (plan, prior year) are greys.
- Put the unit in the chart title, for example "Combined ratio by line, %".

## Footer

Keep or add a small footer on every slide: the source file name and, if the content is illustrative, the label "Illustrative figures for training purposes, not ERGO results". Never remove an existing illustrative or confidentiality label.

## When you finish

In chat, list what you changed per slide in one short line each, and say that no figures were changed.
